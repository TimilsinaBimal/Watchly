How it works

  1. 1
    Accounts
    Connect Stremio, Trakt, Simkl or MDBList so Watchly can read your history.
  2. 2
    Preferences
    Pick a language, poster ratings and genres to skip.
  3. 3
    Catalogs
    Choose which rows to show, rename and reorder them.
  4. 4
    Install
    Add the addon to Stremio or Nuvio and your rows appear.

What you get

Trakt & Simkl

Ratings and rewatches sharpen what gets recommended.

Custom catalogs

Name and order your rows however you like.

Genre filtering

Exclude genres you never want to see.

Multi-language

Recommendations in your preferred language.

Rating posters

Posters with ratings from RPDB or Top Posters.

Stremio & Nuvio

One-click install into either app, or copy the link.

Free, no ads

Built and hosted by one person. If Watchly helps you, you can chip in. Entirely optional.

Made to self-host

Run your own

Watchly is built to run on your own server, not only as a hosted service. One click on Vercel, or Docker next to your other apps.
